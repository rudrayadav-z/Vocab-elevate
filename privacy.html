Privacy Policy

Vocario website and Vocario browser extension · Last updated 25 October 2026

Vocario is a free vocabulary app. This page explains what information the Vocario website (vocab-elevate.pages.dev) and the Vocario browser extension collect, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we keep only what's needed to save your words and run the leaderboard, we never sell anything, and there are no ads or tracking.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Your email address (or your Google account's email, if you sign in with Google)To create your account and let you sign in on any device.
Your usernameShown on the weekly leaderboard to other signed-in users.
The words you save, with their meanings, examples, synonyms and antonyms, and your review historyTo build your flashcards and schedule reviews.
Points you earn, and whenFor levels, streaks and the weekly leaderboard.
Your chosen theme (look and light/dark mode)So the app looks the same on all your devices.

We do not collect your browsing history, the pages you visit, your location, contacts, or payment details. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

The browser extension

  • The extension runs on web pages so that it can show a Look up button when you select a word. It only reads the word you select. It does not read, store or send the rest of the page, and it does not record which sites you visit.
  • When you click Look up, only that word is sent to the free dictionary services listed below to fetch its meaning.
  • When you click Save, the word and its meaning are saved to your Vocario account.
  • The extension stores your sign-in session, your theme and recent lookups in your browser's extension storage, so you stay signed in and repeat lookups are fast.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase stores your account, saved words and points. It is our database and sign-in provider.
  • Google, only if you choose "Sign in with Google".
  • Dictionary services (Datamuse, Wiktionary and Free Dictionary API) receive the single word you look up, and nothing about you.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts the website uses.

We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't use it for anything other than running Vocario.

Your data on your device

The website keeps a copy of your cards in your browser so that reviews work offline. You can download all of it at any time with Desk → Export backup.

Deleting your data

  • Desk → Delete everything removes all your saved words from your device and from our database.
  • To delete your whole account, including your email, username and points, email us at the address below and we'll do it within 7 days.
  • Removing the extension from your browser deletes everything it stored on your computer.

Security

Your data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules make sure each person can only read and change their own words and points. Other users can see only your username and weekly points on the leaderboard.

Children

Vocario is meant for students and learners of all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
